### A new-production replacement power supply board for Gottlieb System 80 and 80A pinball machines

## Introduction

Original System 80/80A power supply boards are over 40 years old now, and failures are common — cracked traces, tired capacitors, and burnt-out regulators. This is a new-production replacement board built to solve that, with all parts through-hole soldered so it's easy to service down the road if it's ever needed.

It's a fit for games like Amazon Hunt, Ready Aim Fire, Super Orbit, The Amazing Spider-Man, Royal Flush Deluxe, Rack 'Em Up, and Q*bert's Quest, among other System 80/80A titles.
