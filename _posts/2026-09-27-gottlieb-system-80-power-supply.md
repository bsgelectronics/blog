### A new replacement power supply board for Gottlieb System 80 and 80A pinball machines

## Introduction

Original System 80/80A power supply boards are over 40 years old and have multiple common failure points that are likely to affect your machine.  Troubleshooting usually inolves finding the manual, holding a flashlight, and testing various points with your mutimeter.  This board should be reliable for many years to come, but makes troubleshooting easy with multiple status leds and labled test points.

## Installation

1. Power down the machine and unplug it from the wall.
2. Locate power supply board.
3. Note the orientation and connector positions, (Take a picture now in case you forget!)
4. Disconnect all connectors from the original board.
5. Remove mounting screws and take out the old board.
6. Mount the new board in the same location using the new screws and standoffs.
7. Reconnect all connectors, matching them to their original orientation.
8. Connect grounding mod wires to the GND connector.
9. Power on the machine and enjoy.
